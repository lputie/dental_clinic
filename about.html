Clinic profile

Publicly available information

Elite Dental Astana is presented online as a dental clinic in Astana. The official website describes a focus on endodontics, including treatment and retreatment of root canals under a microscope. It also describes restoration, implantation and other dental directions.

The organization shown on the official website is ТОО "Elite Dental Astana". The published site gives BIN 230240013342 and license number №24030765, with a stated license issue date of 17 October 2024.
